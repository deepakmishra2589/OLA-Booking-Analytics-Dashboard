# 🚖 OLA Booking Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end Power BI dashboard built using the OLA Booking dataset. The dashboard provides business insights into booking performance, revenue generation, vehicle utilization, booking status, and customer & driver ratings.

The objective of this project is to demonstrate business intelligence, data visualization, and DAX skills by transforming raw booking data into interactive dashboards that support data-driven decision-making.

# 📈 Key Business Insights

- Generated over **103K bookings** during the analysis period.
- Total revenue exceeded **₹35 Million**.
- Overall booking success rate is **62.09%**.
- Cancellation rate stands at **37.91%**, indicating opportunities for operational improvements.
- Prime Sedan is the highest-performing vehicle based on revenue.
- Cash is the most preferred payment method.
- Average booking value is approximately **₹549**.
- Average ride distance is around **14 km**.
- Driver and customer ratings indicate strong service quality.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

- Improve driver allocation to reduce booking cancellations.
- Promote digital payment methods through offers and cashback campaigns.
- Increase availability of high-performing vehicles such as Prime Sedan.
- Focus on high-demand pickup locations to improve booking success.
- Continue monitoring customer and driver ratings to maintain service quality.
